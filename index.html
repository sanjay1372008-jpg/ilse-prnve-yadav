Travel with care

Beautiful places
deserve care.

Island environments are precious. We encourage travellers to learn about the places they visit and to consider how their choices affect local communities and fragile ecosystems.

Respect marine lifeFollow local guidance around reefs and wildlife; never disturb animals.
Support local makersExplore locally owned businesses, guides and craftspeople.
Reduce your footprintConsider waste, water use and lower-impact activities where possible.

These are travel principles, not a claim that any particular property has been independently certified.
